A few helpful notes

Before you set off.

Can I change the dates in a sample package?

The packages on Wanderly are examples for exploring the site. No reservation is made, but you can use the itinerary planner to sketch out dates that work for you.

Are the hotel prices and flight schedules live?

No. Hotel rates, flight details, and package prices shown across this project are sample information for demonstration only.

Does this form send my message?

No. Submitting the form stores the message in local storage in this browser. Nothing is sent to an inbox or external service.
